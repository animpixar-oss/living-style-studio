Our Methodology

From first consultation to final key handover

A rigorous five-stage delivery workflow ensuring zero site surprises and punctual project completion.

01

Consult

A senior designer visits your space to evaluate layout needs, storage routines, and budget parameters.

02

Measure

Laser millimeter precision measurements so every carcass fits your walls, preventing fillers or gaps.

03

3D Design

Realistic photorealistic walkthroughs and finish pairings approved by you before a single board is cut.
